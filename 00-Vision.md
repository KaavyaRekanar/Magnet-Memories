# Magnet Memories

> **Tap a magnet. Relive the journey.**

---

# Vision

Magnet Memories is an offline-first interactive family memory display that transforms physical travel magnets into living stories.

Instead of searching through thousands of photographs on a phone, a family member simply picks up a travel magnet, taps it against the display, and instantly revisits that journey through photographs, Live Photos, and personal memories.

Magnet Memories is designed to preserve experiences, not merely store media.

---

# Why Magnet Memories Exists

Modern families capture thousands of photographs every year.

Ironically, the easier it becomes to take photographs, the harder it becomes to revisit them.

Most memories disappear into cloud storage, scattered across phones, hard drives and online albums. While those systems excel at storing media, they rarely encourage families to relive meaningful experiences together.

Travel magnets tell a different story.

A simple magnet on a refrigerator or notice board often becomes a lasting reminder of a place that mattered. Every time someone notices it, they remember a holiday, a funny moment or a shared experience.

Magnet Memories combines the permanence of physical keepsakes with the richness of digital media.

Each travel magnet becomes a physical key that unlocks an entire journey.

Instead of opening an application, searching for an album and scrolling through hundreds of photographs, users simply tap a magnet and immediately begin reliving that experience.

The objective is not to organise photographs.

The objective is to preserve memories.

# Core Philosophy

Magnet Memories is built around a small number of principles that guide every design and engineering decision.

## 1. Physical First

Physical interaction is the primary user interface.

Users interact with magnets, not menus.

The NFC tag is not a shortcut.

It is the key that unlocks a memory.

Technology should disappear behind the experience.

---

## 2. Memories Before Media

Magnet Memories exists to preserve experiences.

Photographs, Live Photos, videos, maps and written memories are simply different ways of telling a story.

Technology should support memories, never become the focus.

---

## 3. Offline First

Every imported journey should continue to work without:

* Internet access
* Cloud services
* External hard drives
* External servers

Families should always own and access their memories.

---

## 4. Calm by Default

Magnet Memories should feel peaceful.

The interface should resemble a framed photograph or a museum exhibit rather than a modern dashboard.

Design principles include:

* Large imagery
* Restrained typography
* Generous spacing
* Gentle transitions
* Minimal controls
* No visual clutter

The software should quietly disappear into the background, allowing the memories to take centre stage.

---

## 5. Family Centred

Magnet Memories is designed to live inside a home.

It should be intuitive for:

* Children
* Parents
* Grandparents
* Visitors

No technical knowledge should ever be required to enjoy the experience.

---

## 6. Long-Term Preservation

Magnet Memories is intended to preserve memories for decades.

The project should minimise dependence on proprietary online services wherever possible.

Journeys should remain portable using open, documented formats.

Families should always retain ownership of their memories.

# Product Pillars

## Immersion

Opening a journey should feel like stepping back into that moment.

Motion, imagery, typography and transitions should support the emotional experience of remembering rather than simply browsing photographs.

---

## Simplicity

The ideal interaction is:

1. Pick up a magnet.
2. Tap the display.
3. Relive the journey.

Nothing more should be required.

---

## Permanence

Imported journeys belong to the family.

They should remain accessible regardless of future changes to cloud providers, subscriptions or online services.

---

## Delight

Small details matter.

A Live Photo gently coming to life.

A carefully chosen cover photograph.

A meaningful sentence that instantly brings back a forgotten memory.

# Memory Cards

Every journey begins with a **Memory Card**.

A Memory Card is a short, personal introduction written by the family before the slideshow begins.

Rather than immediately displaying photographs, Magnet Memories first presents the story behind the journey.

A Memory Card may include:

* Journey title
* Travel dates
* Location
* A favourite quote
* A short personal reflection

For example:

> *Our first family road trip across the United States. We wandered through Central Park, finally visited the bakeries we'd been talking about for months, and laughed when Niagara Falls completely ignored our waterproof jackets. Looking back, this was the trip where Ira wanted to walk everywhere instead of sitting in the stroller.*

The Memory Card transforms a photo album into a story.

It captures what photographs alone often cannot.

After a short pause—or immediately if the user taps the screen—the slideshow begins.

Every journey follows the same simple structure.

```text
Journey

├── Cover
├── Memory Card
├── Slideshow
├── Live Photos
└── Return Home
```

The experience should feel less like opening a gallery and more like opening a treasured scrapbook.

# Success Criteria

Magnet Memories is successful if:

* Families regularly revisit old journeys.
* Children naturally explore family memories.
* Guests immediately understand how to use it.
* Journeys remain accessible without an internet connection.
* Importing a new journey becomes part of returning home from a holiday.
* Families begin writing Memory Cards because they recognise their long-term value.

---

# Non-Goals

Magnet Memories is **not** intended to become:

* A cloud backup service
* A social media platform
* A photo editing application
* A digital asset management system
* A travel planning application
* A cloud gallery
* An AI photo organiser

Those products already exist.

Magnet Memories exists for one purpose:

> **To transform physical keepsakes into interactive family memories.**

---

# Design Principles

Every feature added to Magnet Memories should satisfy the following questions.

### Does it encourage remembering?

If not, it probably does not belong.

### Can a child use it?

If the interaction is confusing, it should be redesigned.

### Will it still work in twenty years?

If the feature depends on a proprietary cloud service, it should have an offline alternative.

### Does it reduce complexity?

Magnet Memories should become simpler over time, not more complicated.

### Does it respect the user's memories?

The application should never modify original photographs, overwrite personal data or lock user content into proprietary formats.

---

# Product Values

Magnet Memories values stories over storage.

Every feature should strengthen the emotional connection between people and their memories.

Technology should quietly disappear into the background.

The experience should feel less like browsing a gallery and more like opening a treasured scrapbook.

The best software is software that people stop noticing.
